Verdant AI - Brand Identity Assets
This repository contains the digital design assets for the corporate identity of Verdant AI, featuring the official business card front and back mockups.  
🏢 Company Profile
Company Name: Verdant AI  
Key Executive: PAYEL THAKUR (CEO & Co-Founder)  
🧰 Asset Breakdown
The design showcases a two-part layout using a sophisticated tech-inspired color scheme of deep forest green and a clean off-white/light green canvas.

Elements: Features a central stylized digital tree with circuit-inspired branch nodes and leaf icons.  
Text: Includes the company name Verdant AI, founder name PAYEL THAKUR, and executive title CEO & Co-Founder.
